# Crochet conventions Loopi writes to (US terms)

## Abbreviations
ch chain · sl st slip stitch · sc single crochet · hdc half double crochet · dc double crochet ·
tr treble crochet · dtr double treble · inc increase (2 sc in one st) · dec decrease (sc2tog) ·
sc2tog / dc2tog · blo back loop only · flo front loop only · fpdc / bpdc front/back post dc ·
MR magic ring · sk skip · st(s) stitch(es) · rep repeat · yo yarn over · RS/WS right/wrong side ·
ch-sp chain space · beg beginning · rnd round · * * repeat instructions between asterisks

## UK to US conversion (always convert to US)
UK dc = US sc · UK htr = US hdc · UK tr = US dc · UK dtr = US tr · UK trtr = US dtr ·
UK "miss" = US "skip" · UK "tension" = US "gauge" · UK "yarn round hook" = US "yarn over"

## Notation
- Rounds: "Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)". Rows: "Row 1: ch 25, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across (24)".
- Count at end in parentheses. Repeats in asterisks: "*sc, inc* around (18)" or "*2 sc, inc* x6 (24)".
- Collapse identical rounds: "Rnds 6-20: sc in each st around (36)".
- Increase rounds in amigurumi typically add 6 per round; decrease rounds subtract 6.
- V-stitch: (dc, ch 1, dc) in the same st or space unless the creator defines it differently.
- Mesh / filet: "ch 1, sk 1" or "ch 2, sk 2" pattern; state the repeat and turning chain.
- Turning chains: ch 1 for sc rows, ch 2 for hdc, ch 3 for dc (say whether it counts as a stitch).
- Colour changes: "change to Colour B" at the last yo of the previous st.
- Garments: sizes as S (M, L, XL) with numbers in the same order; ease and finished measurements in notes.
